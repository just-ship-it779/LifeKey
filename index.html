WHAT YOU GET

Everything you need in one place

LifeKey gives you three core tools designed to work together seamlessly.

Medical Information Card

Create a compact, printable wallet card containing your key medical details and an emergency QR code — ready when you need it most.

Digital Report Vault

Upload and organise medical reports, prescriptions, and scans in one secure place — accessible from any device at any time.

Quick QR Access

Scan the card's QR code to instantly open the full medical record, including all uploaded reports — no login needed.

How to print your foldable card

  1. Click Print / Save as PDF below.
  2. Set orientation to Landscape and paper size to A4.
  3. Set scale to 100% / Actual Size — do not enable "Fit to page".
  4. Turn on Background graphics so card colours print correctly.
  5. Cut around the card outline, then fold along the centre line so the two faces become the front and back of one card.
